Submission guidelines

RTSS workshop accepts contributions based on the following criteria:

  • The submission should be about 4 pages long (excluding references).
  • The review process is single-blind, and at least two reviewers will review the submitted papers based on the novelty, relevance of the workshop topics, and clarity of language. We welcome perspective and conceptual papers as well.
  • The submission should be sent in PDF to trust.workshop@gmail.com by following IEEE manuscript guidelines. You will receive a confirmation email after your submission.
  • Accepted papers will be available on the workshop website. The authors of accepted papers will be invited for oral presentation.

Topics of interest

The workshop topics include, but not limited to:

  • Computational models of robot trust
  • Regulation and new standards for the societal impact of AI and Robotics
  • Trust in Human-Robot and Robot-Robot Interactions
  • Ethics in AI and Robotics
  • Explainable AI for trustworthy robots
  • Interactive social learning in robotics
  • Trust-enabled symbiotic societies
  • Safety in physical HRI
